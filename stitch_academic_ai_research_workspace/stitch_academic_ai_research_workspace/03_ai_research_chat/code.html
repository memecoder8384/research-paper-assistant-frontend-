Marginalization Granularity: Sequence vs. Token

Lewis et al. (2020) introduce two formulations that differ in how they marginalize over the top-K retrieved Wikipedia passages during generation:

  • RAG-Sequence: Conditions the entire generated output sequence on the same single retrieved document, marginalizing at the sequence level via beam search.
  • RAG-Token: Allows each target token to attend to a different retrieved passage, enabling the generator to synthesize facts across multiple documents within one answer.
Lewis et al. (2020) — RAG p. 3

"RAG-Token model can predict each target token based on a different document, combining information across several passages."

Lewis et al. (2020) — RAG p. 6

"RAG-Sequence outperforms RAG-Token on Jeopardy question generation, while RAG-Token achieves higher diversity on MS-MARCO."
