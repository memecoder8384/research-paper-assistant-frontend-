Potential Research Gaps & Future Directions

Underexplored Topic Lewis p. 8 · Wei p. 11

Interleaved Retrieval Within Chain-of-Thought Reasoning

RAG retrieves passages only once before generation, while CoT relies purely on parametric memory for intermediate reasoning steps.

Why This Matters

Multi-hop scientific queries fail when intermediate reasoning steps hallucinate facts not present in the initial top-K retrieval.

Suggested Future Direction

Design an uncertainty-triggered retrieval loop that queries the vector index mid-reasoning whenever token entropy exceeds a threshold.

Contradictory Findings Lewis p. 7 · Bai p. 14

Parametric Prior Override in Aligned Generators

RLHF/Constitutional alignment strengthens model confidence, yet causes generators to ignore newly retrieved contradictory evidence.

Why This Matters

Reduces reliability of RAG systems in fast-evolving medical, legal, and scientific domains where retrieved facts supersede pre-training data.

Suggested Future Direction

Evaluate context-faithfulness fine-tuning objectives that penalize parametric overrides when retrieved passages have high cosine similarity.

Methodological Limitation Vaswani p. 9 · Lewis p. 4

Evaluation Bias Toward Short-Form Exact Match

Benchmarks rely heavily on Exact Match (EM) and BLEU, which fail to measure citation precision or synthesis quality in long-form academic answers.

Why This Matters

Models optimized for EM scores may produce brittle answers that lack nuanced academic caveats or cross-paper synthesis.

Suggested Future Direction

Construct a claim-level attribution benchmark measuring both citation recall and entailment across multi-document literature reviews.

Cross-Lingual Gap Lewis p. 9 · Wei p. 15

Low-Resource Multilingual Dense Indexing

All four studies evaluate primarily on English corpora, leaving cross-lingual retrieval and reasoning performance largely unquantified.

Why This Matters

Non-English scientific literature remains inaccessible to standard English-centric RAG pipelines without translation loss.

Suggested Future Direction

Benchmark multilingual bi-encoders with cross-lingual CoT prompting on regional scientific repositories.
