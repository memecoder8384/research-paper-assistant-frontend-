AI Comparison Overview

Core Similarities

Both papers rely on multi-head self-attention encoder-decoder Transformer backbones and evaluate sequence generation quality on standard NLP benchmarks using BLEU/EM metrics.

Key Architectural Differences

Vaswani et al. (2017) store all knowledge parametrically inside static weights, whereas Lewis et al. (2020) decouple reasoning from factual storage via a hot-swappable dense Wikipedia index.

Parameter Paper A — Vaswani et al. (2017) Paper B — Lewis et al. (2020)
Research Objective Replace recurrent and convolutional sequence models with pure self-attention for parallelizable sequence transduction. Augment pre-trained seq2seq Transformers with non-parametric dense retrieval for knowledge-intensive NLP tasks.
Problem Statement Sequential RNN computation prevents parallelization across training examples and struggles with long-range dependencies. Parametric LLMs cannot easily update factual knowledge, lack provenance, and hallucinate on rare entities.
Methodology 6-layer encoder-decoder Transformer using scaled dot-product multi-head attention and sinusoidal positional encodings. DPR bi-encoder retriever + FAISS MIPS index paired with BART-large generator, marginalized via RAG-Sequence & RAG-Token.
Dataset WMT 2014 English-German (4.5M sentence pairs) and English-French (36M pairs), plus WSJ Constituency Parsing. Natural Questions, TriviaQA, WebQuestions, CuratedTrec, MS-MARCO, and FEVER over a 21M-passage Wikipedia dump.
Key Findings Achieved 28.4 BLEU (EN-DE) and 41.8 BLEU (EN-FR) at a fraction of the training FLOPs of prior RNN ensembles. Achieved 44.5 EM on Natural Questions; hot-swapping the index updates temporal facts without retraining weights.
Limitations Quadratic O(n²) memory complexity with respect to sequence length; fixed context window during inference. Additional vector search latency and index memory footprint; retrieval errors propagate directly to generation.
Future Scope Extend self-attention to local/restricted attention for images, audio, and video modalities. Jointly pre-train retriever and generator from scratch and support multi-hop iterative retrieval.
